About

Why We Conduct

We're a small collective of perfumers and chemists who treat fragrance the way a conductor treats an orchestra: every ingredient is an instrument, and the composition only works when each one enters at the right moment.

Our formulas blend natural materials with modern aroma molecules so each scent evolves with your skin through the day — an opening movement, a middle, and a finish that lingers. Made in small batches in Aotearoa New Zealand.
